The fall series

The Fourth Quarter

Twelve Sundays, September 13 – November 29, 2026, on what it looks like to thrive in the later chapters of life: faith, friendship, grief, regret, purpose, and what comes next.

Games are won in the fourth quarter, and nobody sits it out. Come for one or come for all twelve. Every week stands on its own.
