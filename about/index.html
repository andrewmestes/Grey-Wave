Our story

Not a senior ministry. A church.

Built for people in the second half of life, led with them, and aimed at everyone who suspects God is finished with them. He isn't.

Why this, why now

Nearly every new church is designed for young families. So we built a different one.

Every day about ten thousand Americans turn 65. By 2030, every Baby Boomer will be past that line. It is the largest and fastest-growing group of people in the country.

Meanwhile, most new churches are designed for young families, with the lights, the volume, and the pace to match. Older adults get a Tuesday-morning program, if they get anything. Many quietly stop coming. Many more never started.

Grey Wave is a church of its own. We started in September 2026 with twelve people in a borrowed room. We intend to be a lot more than that, and to help start churches like this one wherever people are getting older and wondering what's next.

Who's behind this

Forty-five years of starting churches. This one is personal.

Lance Hurley grew up in a ministry family in Ocala, Florida. In 1981, fresh out of Lincoln Christian College, he became the preacher at the Christian Church of Manteno, Illinois, on the church's first birthday. There were about twenty people. Nineteen years later there were 250, and more than 400 people had been baptized.

He went on to serve two churches in Florida, planted Cornerstone Christian Church in Glen Ellyn in 2005, and now leads Ignite Church Planting, which has helped start forty churches across Chicagoland. He and Darla live in Bourbonnais and have been married 42 years.

After a lifetime of starting churches for other people, Lance kept noticing who was left out: his own friends. So he and Darla decided to build a church for their own generation.

"If you ever start a church, I'll come."A longtime friend of Lance's who had said no to church for years. He was there the first Sunday.
What we're about

Grey Wave exists to help people over 55 thrive in the second half of life: with Jesus, with each other, and with something worth getting up for.

Faith that still grows

Whether you've followed Jesus for sixty years or never opened a Bible, there's more. Plain teaching, honest questions, and a God who isn't finished with you.

Friends who show up

People who know your name, notice when you're missing, and come to the hospital when the news is bad. A table you're expected at.

Purpose that outlasts you

You were made with a particular set of gifts, on purpose. We'll help you find them and pour them into grandkids, neighbors, and people who need exactly what you have.

"They will still bear fruit in old age; they will stay fresh and green."

Psalm 92:14

What we believe

Simple faith. Nothing hidden.

Grey Wave is an independent Christian church in the same family as Ignite Church Planting and hundreds of Christian churches across Illinois and Indiana. The idea is two hundred years old: get back to the simple church you read about in the New Testament.

"In essentials, unity. In non-essentials, liberty. In all things, love."
Jesus alone is Savior and Lord.

Not a denomination, not a preacher, not a program. Everything we do points back to him.

The Bible is our guide.

Where it speaks clearly, we speak. Where it's quiet, we leave room for each other.

No creed but Christ.

We ask people to trust and follow Jesus, not to sign a statement.

Baptism, whenever you're ready.

By immersion, the way the New Testament describes it, for anyone old enough to choose it. It is never too late, and there will be plenty of steady hands.

Communion every Sunday.

The Lord's table is the center of our gathering, not an add-on.

Led by our own people.

Elders chosen from among us, answerable to no headquarters.
