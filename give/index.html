Give

You don't have to. Here's how, if you want to.

New churches are built by the people who believe in them. Every gift keeps the coffee on, the rides running, and the doors open for the next person who thinks God is finished with them.

Online

One-time or monthly, by bank transfer or card. You'll get an email receipt and a year-end statement.

Online giving opens soon. Until then, a check or the welcome table works.

By check

Make it payable to Grey Wave Church. Hand it to someone at the welcome table on Sunday, or ask us for the mailing address. No plate is passed during the service.

IRA, stock, or estate

If you're 70½ or older, a qualified charitable distribution from your IRA can count toward your required minimum distribution. Talk to your advisor, then contact us for the details your custodian needs.

Where it goes

Every dollar has a job.

Sunday

The room, the sound, the large-print notes, and the coffee.

Care

Rides, meals, and visits for people in a hard season.

Leadership

So this church gets the full attention it deserves.

The next one

Helping start another church like this, somewhere else people are getting older and wondering what's next.

Questions about giving

Straight answers.

Do I have to give to belong?

No. Nobody is asked, and nobody is watching. Give when you're ready, if you're ready.

Is my gift tax-deductible?

We'll confirm the church's tax status and receipting details here before online giving opens. Ask us anytime.

Can I set up a monthly gift?

Yes, once online giving opens, and it helps a new church plan. You'll be able to change or stop it anytime.

Who handles the money?

We're committed to a finance team of more than one person, with nobody counting alone, and books that anyone who gives can ask to see.

Thank you.

Whatever you give, you're helping someone find their people and their purpose in the second half of life.
